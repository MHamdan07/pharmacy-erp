# 05 Customer Module & E-Storefront

## Overview
The Customer Module encompasses patient relationship management (CRM) inside the back-office and the public e-commerce storefront at `/store`.

## Features
- **Public E-Storefront (`/store`)**: Browse medicines by category pills (Tablets, Capsules, Syrups, Baby Care), search by generic name, and view stock availability.
- **Slide-out Cart Drawer**: Manage quantities, view subtotal, tax calculation, and attach Rx prescription files.
- **Checkout & Payment Gateways**: Cash on Delivery (COD), JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Credit/Debit Card.
- **Order Tracking Timeline**: Real-time status update timeline (`Placed` -> `Pharmacist Verified` -> `Packed` -> `Out for Delivery` -> `Delivered`).
- **Patient CRM**: Track purchase history, loyalty reward points, and store credit balances.
