# 18 AI Module & Machine Learning Studio

## Overview
The AI Module integrates artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms into daily pharmacy operations to improve patient safety, prevent stockouts, and streamline prescription processing.

## Subsystems
- **AI Prescription OCR Engine**: Computer vision prescription handwriting/printed text reader with confidence metrics.
- **Drug-Drug Interaction (DDI) Scanner**: Automatic contraindication scanning during prescription intake and POS checkout.
- **AI 30-Day Demand Forecasting Tool**: Moving average & exponential smoothing demand prediction algorithm.
- **Smart Generic Recommender Tool**: In-stock bio-equivalent generic replacement recommender.
