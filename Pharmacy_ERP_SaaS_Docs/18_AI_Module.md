# 18 AI Module & Machine Learning Studio

## Overview
The AI Module integrates artificial intelligence, computer vision, and machine learning predictive models into daily pharmacy operations to improve clinical safety, prevent stockouts, and automate inventory replenishment.

---

## 13 AI Features & Predictive Models

1. **Medicine OCR**: Computer vision text extraction from medicine packaging labels, SKUs, and expiration dates.
2. **Prescription OCR**: High-accuracy optical character recognition parsing doctor handwriting, drug names, and frequencies.
3. **Medicine Detection**: Artificial intelligence identification of physical pills, dosage forms, and brand packaging.
4. **Alternative Medicine Recommendation**: 1-click in-stock bio-equivalent generic substitute recommender.
5. **Stock Forecasting**: Moving average inventory depletion modeling calculating days of stock remaining.
6. **Demand Prediction**: Exponential smoothing algorithm projecting future drug demand based on historical POS sales.
7. **Sales Prediction**: Seasonal sales trend forecasting for upcoming weeks and months.
8. **Expiry Prediction**: Predictive risk matrix identifying inventory batches approaching expiration windows.
9. **Drug Interaction Warning**: Automated pharmacological contraindication scanner flagging high/moderate/low risk drug pairs.
10. **Medicine Recommendation**: Cross-selling and complementary therapy suggestions (e.g. Probiotics with Antibiotics).
11. **Customer Purchase Prediction**: Predicts recurring chronic disease patient refill due dates.
12. **Automatic Reorder Suggestions**: Automated purchase order generation when stock falls below AI-computed reorder points.
13. **AI Chat Assistant**: Interactive natural language AI assistant responding to stock velocity and clinical queries.

---

## AI Interfaces & Tools

- **AI Dashboard**: Visual KPI hub presenting real-time demand forecasts, critical reorder alerts, and interaction warnings.
- **AI Reports**: Specialized predictive analytics reports with 1-click Excel and PDF exports.
