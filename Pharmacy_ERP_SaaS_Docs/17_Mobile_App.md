# 17 Mobile App Suite & PWA Ecosystem

## Overview
The Mobile App Suite provides cross-platform Progressive Web Applications (PWA) and React Native mobile applications tailored for two distinct end-user groups: **Customer Patients** and **Pharmacy Employees**.

---

## 1. Customer Mobile App (10 Core Features)

1. **Medicine Search**: Real-time drug lookup by generic ingredient or commercial brand name.
2. **Barcode Scanner**: Camera-based 1D/2D product barcode scanner for instant item verification.
3. **Prescription Upload**: Direct mobile camera capture and file uploader for Rx prescriptions.
4. **Notifications**: Push notification engine for order status updates, prescription approvals, and refill alerts.
5. **Order Tracking**: Real-time GPS delivery status timeline (`Order Placed` -> `Verified` -> `Packed` -> `Out for Delivery` -> `Delivered`).
6. **Wishlist**: Save favorite and recurring chronic disease medications for 1-click reorder.
7. **Medicine Reminder**: Daily dosage alarms with sound and push notifications.
8. **Payments**: Integrated payment gateways supporting Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Cards.
9. **Customer Support**: In-app customer support live chat and hotline dialer.
10. **Dark Mode & Offline History**: Native dark medical theme and offline cached purchase history.

---

## 2. Employee Mobile App (8 Core Features)

1. **Inventory**: Mobile stock lookup, batch inspection, and physical stock count adjustments.
2. **Sales**: Mobile sales ledger tracking daily revenue and transaction volume.
3. **POS**: Touch-friendly mobile POS billing for floor staff and quick checkout.
4. **Prescription**: Prescription queue review and AI OCR inspection studio on mobile devices.
5. **Branch Dashboard**: Mobile branch KPIs displaying sales, low stock alerts, and revenue metrics.
6. **Attendance**: Geo-fenced clock-in and clock-out employee shift attendance logging.
7. **Notifications**: Real-time alerts for low stock thresholds, 30-day batch expiry, and transfer requests.
8. **Reports**: Executive mobile analytics summaries with PDF report view.
