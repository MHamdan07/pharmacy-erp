# 15 Reports & Analytics Studio

## Overview
The Reports Module provides comprehensive financial, operational, and inventory analytics with 1-click Excel (ExcelJS) and PDF (PDFKit) exports.

## Analytics Views
- **Sales & Revenue Report**: Total sales, gross profit margin, tax collected.
- **Top-Selling Drugs Chart**: Ranked by sales volume, revenue contribution, and profit margin using Recharts visual charts.
- **Stock Valuation**: Total stock value at purchase cost vs retail price.
- **Expiry Risk Assessment Matrix**: Identifies items expiring within 30/60/90 days to minimize waste.
- **1-Click Export Engines**: Download structured `.xlsx` spreadsheets or vector PDF reports.
