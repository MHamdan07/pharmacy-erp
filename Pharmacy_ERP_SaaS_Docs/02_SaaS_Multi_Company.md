# 02 SaaS Multi-Company & Tenant Management

## Overview
The Multi-Company SaaS Module governs tenant registration, multi-branch hierarchy, custom branding, domain aliases, and tenant-level subscription billing.

## Core Capabilities
- **Multi-Tenant Isolation**: Every pharmacy enterprise operates in a completely segregated tenant workspace identified by `pharmacy` ObjectId.
- **Corporate Profile Settings**: Business name, registration tax ID (NTN/VAT), custom logo upload, hotline, and thermal receipt footer.
- **Custom Branding Tokens**: Custom accent colors, currency symbols (`$`, `PKR`, `AED`), and localized date formats.
- **Subscription Lifecycle**: Automatic subscription status checks, plan upgrade/downgrade, and billing invoice history.
