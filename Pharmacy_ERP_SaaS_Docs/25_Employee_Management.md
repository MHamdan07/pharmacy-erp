# 25 Employee Management & Staff Shifts

## Overview
The Employee Management Module oversees staff profiles, shift scheduling, role assignment, attendance logs, and staff performance KPIs.

## Core Features
- **Employee Roster**: Staff directory containing full name, email, phone, role (Manager, Pharmacist, Cashier, Inventory Staff), assigned branch, and employment status.
- **Shift Scheduling**: Assign morning, evening, or night shifts to employees per branch.
- **Role Permission Assignment**: Dynamically customize access permissions per employee.
- **Sales Performance Tracking**: Monitor sales revenue and invoice counts generated per cashier and pharmacist.
