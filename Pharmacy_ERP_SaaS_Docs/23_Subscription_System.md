# 23 Subscription System & SaaS Billing

## Overview
The Subscription System manages SaaS tier levels, feature gates, recurring billing status, and tenant plan limits.

## Tier Breakdown
- **Basic Plan ($49/mo)**: 1 Branch, 3 Users, POS Billing, Thermal Receipts, FEFO Inventory, Standard Reports.
- **Growth Plan ($129/mo)**: Up to 3 Branches, 10 Users, POS + E-Storefront (`/store`), AI OCR Prescription Scans (500 scans/mo), Inter-Branch Transfers.
- **Enterprise Plan ($299/mo)**: Unlimited Branches, Unlimited Users, AI Demand Forecasting Engine, Unlimited OCR Scans, Custom Branding, Dedicated Backups.
