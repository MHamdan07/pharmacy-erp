# 13 Pharmacist Module & Clinical Workstation

## Overview
The Pharmacist Module empowers licensed clinical staff to verify patient prescriptions, inspect AI OCR results, review drug-drug interaction warnings, check dosage safety parameters, and approve prescription orders prior to dispensing.

## Workstation Features
- **Prescription Queue**: Real-time listing of uploaded prescriptions from walk-in patients and e-storefront orders.
- **AI OCR Inspection Studio**: Side-by-side view of prescription image and extracted medications text with confidence metrics.
- **Drug-Drug Interaction Scanner**: Severe/Moderate/Mild warning alerts.
- **Generic Substitutions**: 1-click bio-equivalent generic replacement recommendation.
- **Digital Sign-Off**: Attaches license number, timestamp, and signature to unlock items for POS checkout.
