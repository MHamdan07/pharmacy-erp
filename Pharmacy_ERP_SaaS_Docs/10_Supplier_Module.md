# 10 Supplier Management & Vendor Ledger

## Overview
The Supplier Module manages pharmaceutical distributors, manufacturing vendors, credit terms, contact profiles, and vendor performance history.

## Directory & Features
- **Supplier Profile**: Company name, contact person, phone, email, tax registration ID, address, credit limit, and payment terms.
- **Purchase History**: Complete record of historical purchase orders placed with vendor.
- **Payables Summary**: Total outstanding balance owed per supplier with aging breakdown (0-30 days, 30-60 days, 60+ days).
