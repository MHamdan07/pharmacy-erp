# 11 Sales Module & Transaction History

## Overview
The Sales Module tracks all retail POS sales, e-commerce storefront orders, prescription billing transactions, shift closings, and sales returns.

## Capabilities
- **Transaction Ledger**: Complete list of completed, pending, and refunded sales with invoice ID, cashier name, customer name, branch, payment method, and total amount.
- **Invoice Re-printing**: Generate thermal or PDF receipt duplicates.
- **Shift Register Closing (Z-Report)**: Compare expected cash drawer total against physical cash count at shift end.
- **Returns & Exchanges**: Process item returns, log return reasons, and restock non-damaged goods back into active batch inventory.
