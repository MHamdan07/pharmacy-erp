# 03 Website Homepage & ERP SaaS Landing Portal

## Overview
The ERP SaaS Homepage serves as the central public marketing landing page for the **Pharmacy ERP Platform**. It is strictly distinct from the retail patient customer storefront located at `/store`.

---

## 8 Core Homepage Sections

### 1. Hero Section
- **SaaS Headline**: "Pharmacy ERP SaaS — Manage your pharmacy business from one platform"
- **Subheadline**: Streamline multi-company operations, multi-branch inventory, POS billing, AI prescription OCR, and financial analytics.
- **Call-To-Actions (CTAs)**:
  - `Start Free Trial` -> Opens tenant registration page `/register-tenant`.
  - `Book Demo` -> Opens interactive demo modal / owner portal `/login`.

### 2. Features Overview Grid
- **Multi-Company Support**: Manage independent corporate entities and multi-tenant billing.
- **Multi-Branch Management**: Centralized headquarters control over retail outlets and warehouses.
- **Inventory Tracking**: FEFO batch tracking, automated reorder thresholds, and expiration alerts.
- **Prescription Management**: AI OCR text extraction, DDI interaction warnings, and pharmacist digital sign-off.
- **Billing & POS**: High-speed 1D/2D barcode checkout, split payments, and ESC/POS thermal receipts.
- **Supplier Management**: Purchase orders, vendor ledgers, batch receiving, and payables.
- **AI-Powered Insights**: 30-day demand forecasting and generic bio-equivalent substitution.
- **Reports & Analytics**: Real-time sales KPIs, COGS profit margins, Excel & PDF export engines.
- **Mobile App**: Delivery agent GPS route app, manager mobile portal, and PWA patient storefront.
