# 00 Project Overview

## Introduction
The **Pharmacy ERP & Inventory Management System** is an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant SaaS solution designed to power independent pharmacies, hospital pharmacy chains, and multi-branch retail medical stores.

## Purpose
To streamline pharmaceuticals inventory tracking, point-of-sale billing, FEFO batch control, prescription intake, supplier purchase orders, and AI-driven forecasting within a secure, high-performance platform.

## System Actors & Main Users
- **Super Admin**: Platform oversight, SaaS tenant subscriptions, global analytics, system maintenance.
- **Company Owner**: Full tenant control, multi-branch setup, subscription billing, executive reports.
- **Branch Admin / Manager**: Branch-level inventory, stock transfers, employee shifts, local sales approval.
- **Pharmacist**: Prescription review & pricing, dosage verification, drug interaction checking, stock deduction.
- **Cashier**: POS checkout, barcode/QR scanning, cash/card payment collection, invoice generation.
- **Inventory Staff**: Purchase order receiving, batch creation, stock intake, barcode labeling, expiry checks.
- **Delivery Staff**: Order dispatch, delivery routing, proof-of-delivery capture, status updates.
- **Customer / Patient**: Public storefront browsing, online order placement, prescription upload, delivery tracking.

## Core Module Map
1. Multi-Tenant SaaS Architecture & Onboarding
2. Multi-Branch Operations & Inter-Branch Stock Transfers
3. FEFO Batch Inventory Management & Expiry Control
4. POS Billing & Thermal Receipt Printing
5. AI Prescription OCR & Alternative Medicine Recommendations
6. Customer Storefront & Mobile Order Tracking
7. Financial Accounting, Audit Logs & 2FA Security
